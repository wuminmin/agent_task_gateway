\section{Complete V2 Inference Rules}

This section contains the full typing and big-step rules summarized in the
main paper. Undefined premises reject the plan. Join fields are stable
role-qualified IDs; equality requires the same canonical type and collation;
union schemas are identical including type and collation.

\subsection{Typing}

\begingroup\small
\begin{align*}
\frac{D(\nu,s):\Gamma\quad \operatorname{canonical}_\Pi(\Gamma,r)}
 {\Pi\vdash\operatorname{Scan}[\nu,s,r,\Gamma]:\Gamma}
&\;(T\text{-Scan})\\[1mm]
\frac{\Pi\vdash e:\Gamma\quad F\subseteq\operatorname{dom}\Gamma
      \quad \Pi;\Gamma\vdash p:\mathbb B_3}
 {\Pi\vdash\sigma_{p;F}(e):\Gamma}
&\;(T\text{-Select})\\[1mm]
\frac{\Pi\vdash e:\Gamma\quad \varnothing\ne A\subseteq\operatorname{dom}\Gamma}
 {\Pi\vdash\pi_A(e):\Gamma|_A}
&\;(T\text{-Project})\\[1mm]
\frac{\Pi\vdash e_i:\Gamma_i\;(i=1,2)\quad
      \operatorname{dom}\Gamma_1\cap\operatorname{dom}\Gamma_2=\varnothing
      \quad\operatorname{eqtyped}_\Pi(\Theta)}
 {\Pi\vdash e_1\bowtie_\Theta e_2:\Gamma_1\uplus\Gamma_2}
&\;(T\text{-Join})\\[1mm]
\frac{\Pi\vdash e_1:\Gamma\quad\Pi\vdash e_2:\Gamma
      \quad\operatorname{distinctable}_\Pi(\Gamma)}
 {\Pi\vdash e_1\cup_\delta e_2:\Gamma}
&\;(T\text{-Union})\\[1mm]
\frac{\Pi\vdash e:\Gamma\quad G\subseteq\operatorname{dom}\Gamma
      \quad |G|+|\mathcal A|>0\quad
      \operatorname{distinctable}_\Pi(G)\quad
      \Pi;\Gamma\vdash\mathcal A:\Gamma_{\mathcal A}}
 {\Pi\vdash\gamma_{G;\mathcal A}(e):\Gamma|_G\uplus\Gamma_{\mathcal A}}
&\;(T\text{-Group})\\[1mm]
\frac{\Pi\vdash e:\Gamma\quad o,l\in\mathbb N\quad
      \Pi;\Gamma\vdash O{+}K:\operatorname{TotalOrder}}
 {\Pi\vdash\operatorname{Page}_{O,K,o,l}(e):\Gamma}
&\;(T\text{-Page}).
\end{align*}
\endgroup

\subsection{Big-Step Evaluation}

Write $\alpha_r(t)=(S_r,W_r)$ and $\alpha_a(t)=(S_a,W_a)$.
$\operatorname{aug}_F(t)$ replaces $\alpha_r(t)$ by
$\alpha_r(t)\oplus\bigoplus_{a\in F}\alpha_a(t)$ and otherwise copies the
cell map. A derived cell is
$\langle v_a,\tau_a,\alpha_a,x_a,\rho_a\rangle$. Each displayed bag is paired
with its statically derived schema and source/snapshot bundle. Every conclusion
is followed by the representation validator; failure removes the derivation.

\begingroup\small
\begin{align*}
[E\text{-Scan}]\quad
&\frac{D(\nu,s)=[(e_i,v_i)]_i}
 {\Pi;D\vdash\operatorname{Scan}[\nu,s,r,\Gamma]\Downarrow
  \lbag\langle e_i,\{a\mapsto\langle v_i[a],\tau_a,\{BC_i^a\},[BC_i^a\mapsto1],\nu.a,BC_i^a\rangle\},
  \{BR_i\},[BR_i\mapsto1],\{(r,\nu,e_i)\}\rangle\rbag_i},\\[-1mm]
&\hspace{5mm}BR_i=f_{row}(\nu,s,e_i),\quad BC_i^a=f_{cell}(\nu,s,e_i,a,\tau_a,v_i[a]).\\[1mm]
[E\text{-Select}]\quad
&\frac{\Pi;D\vdash e\Downarrow R}
 {\Pi;D\vdash\sigma_{p;F}(e)\Downarrow
  R[\operatorname{rows}:=\lbag\operatorname{aug}_F(t)\mid t\in R,
  \llbracket p\rrbracket_{\Pi,t}=\mathsf T\rbag]}.\\[1mm]
[E\text{-Project}]\quad
&\frac{\Pi;D\vdash e\Downarrow R}
 {\Pi;D\vdash\pi_A(e)\Downarrow
  R[\Gamma:=\Gamma|_A,\operatorname{cells}(t):=\operatorname{cells}(t)|_A]}.\\[1mm]
[E\text{-Join}]\quad
&\frac{\Pi;D\vdash e_j\Downarrow R_j\;(j=1,2)}
 {\Pi;D\vdash e_1\bowtie_\Theta e_2\Downarrow
  \lbag J(t_1,t_2)\mid t_j\in R_j,
  \bigwedge_{(a=b)\in\Theta}\llbracket t_1.a=t_2.b\rrbracket_\Pi=\mathsf T\rbag},\\[-1mm]
&\hspace{5mm}J.k=H(\code{join-row},\operatorname{sort}[RID_1,RID_2]),\\[-1mm]
&\hspace{5mm}\alpha_r(J)=\alpha_r(t_1)\oplus\alpha_r(t_2)
  \oplus\!\bigoplus_{(a=b)\in\Theta}(\alpha_a(t_1)\oplus\alpha_b(t_2)),\\[-1mm]
&\hspace{5mm}J.\operatorname{cells}=t_1.\operatorname{cells}\uplus t_2.\operatorname{cells}.\\[1mm]
[E\text{-Union}]\quad
&\frac{\Pi;D\vdash e_j\Downarrow R_j\;(j=1,2)}
 {\Pi;D\vdash e_1\cup_\delta e_2\Downarrow
  \lbag U_Q\mid Q\in(R_1\uplus R_2)/{\equiv_\Gamma}\rbag},\\[-1mm]
&\hspace{5mm}U_Q.k=k_{union}(\Gamma,\operatorname{tuple}(Q)),\quad
  \beta_\Gamma(t)=\alpha_r(t)\oplus\!\bigoplus_{a\in\Gamma}\alpha_a(t),\\[-1mm]
&\hspace{5mm}\alpha_r(U_Q)=\bigsqcup_{t\in Q}\beta_\Gamma(t),\\[-1mm]
&\hspace{5mm}U_Q.\operatorname{cells}(a)=\langle v_a,\tau_a,
  \bigsqcup_{t\in Q}\alpha_a(t),N(\code{union}(x_{t,a})),\rho_a\rangle,\\[-1mm]
&\hspace{5mm}\rho_a=\begin{cases}
\operatorname{mat}(U_Q,a),&\text{if every member materializes to one FactID},\\
\bot,&\text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}\\[1mm]
[E\text{-Group}]\quad
&\frac{\Pi;D\vdash e\Downarrow R}
 {\Pi;D\vdash\gamma_{G;\mathcal A}(e)\Downarrow
  \lbag G_Q\mid Q\in\operatorname{Part}_G(R)\rbag},\\[-1mm]
&\hspace{5mm}G_Q.k=k_{group}(G,Q),\quad
  \alpha_r(G_Q)=\bigoplus_{t\in Q}\left(
  \alpha_r(t)\oplus\!\bigoplus_{g\in G}\alpha_g(t)\right),\\[-1mm]
&\hspace{5mm}G_Q.\operatorname{cells}(g)=\langle v_g,\tau_g,
  \bigoplus_{t\in Q}\alpha_g(t),N(x_g),\operatorname{mat}(G_Q,g)\rangle,\\[-1mm]
&\hspace{5mm}G_Q.\operatorname{cells}(a')=\langle
  \operatorname{PgAgg}_{\Pi}(Q,G,\mathcal A)_{a'},\tau_{a'},
  \bigoplus_{t\in Q}\alpha_{\arg(a')}(t),N(x_{a'}),
  \operatorname{mat}(G_Q,a')\rangle.\\[1mm]
[E\text{-Page}]\quad
&\frac{\Pi;D\vdash e\Downarrow R\quad L=\operatorname{sort}_{\prec_{O,K}}(R)}
 {\Pi;D\vdash\operatorname{Page}_{O,K,o,l}(e)\Downarrow
  R[\operatorname{rows}:=L[b:z]]},\\[-1mm]
&\hspace{5mm}b=\min(o,n),\quad z=\min(n,o+l^*),\quad
  l^*=\begin{cases}n,&l=0,\\l,&l>0.\end{cases}
\end{align*}
\endgroup

Here $\equiv_\Gamma$ is componentwise \code{IS NOT DISTINCT FROM}. Union
support includes every candidate and every distinctness field; pointwise
maximum combines alternative witnesses. Grouping includes group-key inputs and
the complete declared aggregate argument, including NULL and non-extreme
\code{MIN/MAX} inputs. Exact/integer \code{SUM} is admitted; floating-point
\code{SUM} is undefined. Pagination uses PostgreSQL ordering plus a Catalog
key suffix that proves totality, and copies annotations only for the delivered
slice. These are positive-output dependencies, not physical-read or minimal
causal provenance.

\section{V3 Outcome Wrapper}

V3 leaves every V2 release/dependency FactID unchanged. After the V2
observation is normalized, the gateway computes
\[
 h_R=H(\code{TASKGATE-OUTCOME-DIGEST-V1},m,
       \operatorname{sort}\{\operatorname{Canon}(f):f\in F_r\})
\]
and creates one outcome payload
$\langle\code{taskgate-exposure-v3},\code{outcome},
\operatorname{ver}(NF),H(NF(q)),h_R,m\rangle$.
Its hash uses the disjoint \code{TASKGATE-FACT-V3} domain. Thus V3 observations
are triples $(F_r,F_i,F_o)$ with $|F_o|=1$ for every successfully released
query. Failed/pre-execution queries produce no outcome fact. The Control
PostgreSQL transaction inserts the three sets under distinct ledger kinds and
checks all three root-family ceilings before release.

The RQ3 threshold campaign uses three normalized predicates whose global
\code{COUNT(*)} output is the same zero. Their V2 release/dependency increments
after the first are zero, but the three normal-form digests yield three outcome
facts. Exact replay and an alias/case/whitespace rewrite reuse the first fact.

\section{Broad SQL Result-Equivalence Stress Test}

This supplement reports a campaign intentionally separated from the main
paper's exposure-invariance evidence.  It asks only whether each generated
PostgreSQL rewrite returns the same rows as its baseline.  Most templates lie
outside TaskGate's closed structured-plan algebra; therefore the campaign does
\emph{not} establish equal release FactSets, equal positive-output-dependency
FactSets, equal incremental charges, or online compiler coverage.

The generator instantiates \RQTwoTemplates{} structural templates over 128
predicate scenarios and a 12-row fixture.  It emits \RQTwoPairs{} attempts,
collapses SQL whitespace, hashes each ordered statement pair and the sorted
pair set, rejects duplicate normalized pairs, and executes all
\RQTwoUnique{} unique pairs on PostgreSQL~16. Expected rows come from an
independent Go fixture evaluator.  The templates cover predicate reordering,
derived projection, CTE pushdown, De Morgan rewriting, correlated
\code{EXISTS}, a one-row \code{VALUES} join, and two page partitions.
