# Project Instructions

## SessionBound and TKDE version relationship

- Treat `SessionBound: Turning Enterprise Task Approval into Budgeted Database
  Sessions` (arXiv:2607.00751v1) as a preliminary preprint, not as separately
  published prior work.
- The related TDSC working draft was never submitted to a journal or conference
  and is not under review. There is no concurrent-submission issue. Do not
  describe the TDSC draft as withdrawn; the accurate status is never submitted.
- Treat the TKDE TaskGate manuscript as the substantially revised article. Its
  new body of work is the database-specific accounting semantics,
  implementation, formal analysis, and evaluation.
- In the TKDE manuscript, identify and cite arXiv:2607.00751v1 once in the
  Introduction. Do not repeatedly compare SessionBound and TaskGate as if they
  were independent systems. Keep SessionBound out of the Related Work
  comparison table and novelty-boundary comparison.
- When the TKDE manuscript is finalized, disclose the version relationship and
  material differences in the cover letter and arXiv comments. Uploading the
  TKDE manuscript to arXiv creates a later version; it does not erase v1.
