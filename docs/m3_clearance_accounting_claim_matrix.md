# M3 Profile-Relative Clearance Accounting Claim Matrix

The M3 review began at commit
`6b552a4802b64cac8f3a23c68527dfa026991553`; the current matrix reflects
follow-up evidence reviewed through
`8bc2202cbb0960617e17b05994192d93c686a286`. It distinguishes paper semantics,
bounded TLA+ invariants, PostgreSQL transaction structure, and executable
tests. A `SUPPORTED` row means the scoped claim has identified evidence; it
does not mean that one layer proves the complete implementation.

The accepted safety statement is:

> BDG does not estimate total information learned by an agent. Under a
> task-bound, versioned exposure profile, it exactly computes modeled Result,
> Dependency, and Outcome set novelty and prevents those modeled effects from
> crossing the AVAILABLE boundary without atomic root-family settlement.

## Binding vocabulary

The implementation has no receipt fields named `profile_digest`,
`effect_digest`, or `candidate_digest`.

- The receipt directly records `ExposureEvidenceV1.ProfileVersion`,
  `ObservationSHA256`, `DictionarySetSHA256`, and the three component-set
  digests.
- The signed Grant and top-level receipt directly carry Grant and Catalog
  digests.
- The dictionary-set manifest transitively binds the Catalog plus publication,
  manifest, and dictionary identities.
- The production effect/manifest identity is `observation_sha256`; the artifact
  intent identity is `intent_sha256`.

Accordingly, the paper says the receipt **transitively binds** the complete
profile/publication environment; it does not invent a direct profile digest.

## Bounded-model caveats

- `ArtifactPublication.tla` checks an abstract settled flag and canonical hash,
  not effect sets, budgets, receipts, or availability audit.
- `ExposureLedger.tla` and `ExposureBitmapRefinement.tla` check bounded
  settlement/novelty/budget properties but have no artifact state.
- `OutcomeSetAbstractRefinement.tla` checks exact set difference, no double
  charge, budget safety, and a collision fail-closed flag. It does not model
  radix layout, predicate payload separation, replay/retry actions, or general
  corruption.
- The saved TLC results are bounded evidence, not a full-state or
  implementation proof.
